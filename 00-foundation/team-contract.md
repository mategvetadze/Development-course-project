## How we work

- Channel: Instagram group chat (currently in use, transitioning to Discord)  
- Standup: Tuesdays at 11:00 (during lab) and Sundays for weekly synthesis calls; continuous baseline availability required  
- Response window: Messages acknowledged within 3 hours  
- Silent teammate with a deadline near: A teammate failing to respond within 3 hours receives a formal warning. Accumulating 3 warnings triggers immediate escalation to lab mentors for team mediation or removal.  
- Demo-ready: any member can screen-share the current product state in any lab, on 2 minutes notice  

## How we decide

Small stuff: Any two members.  
Technical & Architecture decisions: The Build team (Mate and Ani) collaborates on technical and architectural decisions for execution speed and reports the updates back to the team.  
Major Direction & Pivots: Any critical change or situation that fundamentally alters the project scope must be communicated to the team immediately and requires approval from at least 3 team members.  
Record keeping: From Week 3, product decisions go in `DECISIONS.md` with a link to their evidence. That file is the team memory; keep it honest.

## When it breaks

1. **Early Notification:** Any issue, delay, or technical blocker must be officially posted in the team channel (Instagram/Discord) within 48 hours of noticing it.
2. **Warning System & Accountability:** If a teammate fails to respond within the 3-hour response window or misses a task without prior notice, they receive a formal warning in the chat.
3. **Escalation & Removal:** Accumulating 3 formal warnings, or proposing major scope changes without a 3-member consensus, triggers an internal review. If unresolved within one standup, the team will escalate the case to Zeshan Ahmad (zeshan.ahmad@kiu.edu.ge) for formal course mediation or team removal.

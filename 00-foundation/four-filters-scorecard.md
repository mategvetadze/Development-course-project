Pick: Student job search in Kutaisi · Runner-up: Beginner tutors finding students

Why we picked it despite the lower score: Job search scored lowest (5, against 8 and 7), and it rests on one close observation (Giorgi). We still chose to investigate it for three reasons. 
First, it has the clearest measurable cost in our pool: 98 applications, 30 replies (27 rejections, 3 interviews), 0 offers and 13 weeks without a job. Second, we are all students who will face the same job market soon, and we can already reach five job seekers this week. 
Third, we do not yet know where the problem sits: in the quality of listings (3+ years of experience, 1200 GEL) or in the search process, and interviews are the cheapest way to find out. We are picking it to investigate, not to build. If interviews show that the cause is a shortage of jobs that no product can change, we switch to the runner-up (beginner tutors finding students).



## What interviewees actually use today

| Workaround or alternative (as they named it) | The job it does for them | Logs that mention it | What it fails at, in their words (quote + log number) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn, jobs.ge and other job websites | Find vacancies | 01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 09 | "You have to sort through each posting out of so many, read them, and check across several different websites at the same time." (04) |
| Facebook and Instagram posts, social networks | Find vacancies posted by companies | 01, 07, 09 | "Filtering was hard. On Facebook, many unrelated jobs showed up, and I had to spend most of my time sorting through them." (07) |
| Company websites and pages (TBC, BOG, EPAM) | Find vacancies, courses and openings at one employer | 01, 04, 05 | "Searching is difficult; even though vacancies are public, I always end up missing a large number of them." (05) |
| University information channels and groups | Hear about offers from the university | 01, 03, 05 | No complaint about this source is recorded in the logs. |
| Friends | Hear about vacancies by word of mouth | 01, 05 | No complaint about this source is recorded in the logs. |
| Reading each posting's requirements one by one | Check whether they qualify | 01, 04, 06 | "You have to read through huge lists of requirements just to realize they want someone with 3 years of experience for a "junior" role, which makes it very hard to find the right entry-level spot." (06) |
| Messaging the poster or employer | Ask basic details the post does not give | 07, 08 | "I had to message people just to ask basic things like the salary and the hours." (08) |
| Searching with AI / ChatGPT | Find vacancies faster | 02, 05 | No complaint recorded. 02 says "Searching the specific platforms with AI makes it easier to find the job." (02) |
| Not applying, waiting | Avoid applying to a poor fit or while studying | 02, 06 | "I didn't send my CV anywhere this time, I couldn't find a matching vacancy." (06) |

## The strongest incumbent

Job websites and social networks (LinkedIn, jobs.ge, Facebook, Instagram). They are named in the most logs (01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 09), and nobody says they stopped using them; the complaints are about sorting through too many posts, not about leaving (04, 07).

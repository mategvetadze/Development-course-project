## Full answers (by script question)

1. **Study / year:** First-year CS at KIU; leaning toward backend.
2. **Working / internship:** No official job. Small freelance site for a friend. Wants an internship; unsure first-years get taken.
3. **Where they look:** LinkedIn mostly; sometimes jobs.ge and hr.ge; Telegram and Facebook groups for Georgian IT; sometimes seniors post in the university group chat.
4. **Last check / before that:** Scrolled LinkedIn about two weeks ago; before that about a month earlier when a friend mentioned a company hiring interns.
5. **Device:** Mostly phone between classes; laptop for applying / editing CV.
6. **How they decide relevance:** Title first, then requirements. Closes 2+ years experience posts. Reads further if junior/intern and mentions Java, Python, or Spring. Often unsure if qualified.
7. **Last sit-down search:** About two or three weeks ago, evening after classes, in the dorm.
8. **Hardest part of that search:** “Junior” posts that want experience; hard to know what to apply for; few openings at their level in Georgia; internships unpaid or almost unpaid but full-time hours; posts scattered across LinkedIn / Facebook / Telegram.
9. **How it ended:** Saved two postings, applied to neither. CV felt empty; did not know what to write in a cover letter; postponed and never returned.
10. **Missed too late (past year):** About three or four times. One deadline three days past; one internship a friend mentioned after the window closed.
11. **Applications (past 3 months):** Zero to maybe one — sent one application, no reply.
12. **Tools:** LinkedIn profile + job alerts; turned off attention because alerts were mostly senior/irrelevant. No other tools.

**Workaround:** Checks LinkedIn, jobs.ge, hr.ge, Telegram/Facebook groups, and the uni chat by hand. Saves posts instead of applying. Ignores LinkedIn alerts now.

**Cost:** Time checking five places; three or four missed/late discoveries in a year; one application with no reply; two saved posts never submitted.

**Frequency:** Last proper scroll ~2 weeks ago; before that ~1 month earlier (friend tip).

**Prediction check:** Raised on their own (in Q8, before we pitched a product) that there are few openings at their level in Georgia, and that internship pay/hours feel unfair. So for this log, the original “too few / unfair” prediction is **not** killed — this student did raise both. Still also raised the scattered-sources and unclear-eligibility problems that support our Week 4 problem statement.

**Surprise ★:** First-year who wants backend internship but stops at “save for later” because the CV feels empty — the search fails before apply, not only because of missing posts.

**Commitment:** Not recorded.

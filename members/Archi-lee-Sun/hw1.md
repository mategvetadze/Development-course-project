**One moment I broke the Mom Test, and what I asked instead next time (Interview 03):**

In my first interview I used [script v1, question 10](../../01-discovery/interview-script-v1.md): “Over the past year, how many times has it happened that you found out about an interesting job opportunity too late or missed the application deadline?” Asking for a yearly count pushed toward a vague estimate and assumed the problem was a vacancy or deadline. The student instead said they did not forget deadlines but had missed important events. I noticed the problem during the conversation and followed up by asking when it last happened and then about the preceding time. The [log](../../01-discovery/interview-logs/03-student-cs.md) preserves only the last time: “a month or two ago”; it does not preserve an answer about the preceding event. I cannot verify the exact wording I used in later interviews. The later [written script v2](../../01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md) asks for the most recent specific opportunity and what happened instead of a count.
